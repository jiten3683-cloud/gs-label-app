What you need

  • The JBC GS bridge device from JB Corporation
  • A weighing scale with RS-232 output (Mettler Toledo, JBC or any other brand)
  • A TSC / TSPL label printer (203 or 300 dpi)
  • A licence key, supplied with the bridge

Getting started

  1. Connect the scale and printer to the bridge and power it on.
  2. Open the app and enter your licence key and customer ID.
  3. Allow Bluetooth when asked. The app finds and connects to the bridge.
  4. Place an item on the scale, choose a template and tap Print Label.

Common questions

The app shows "Offline"

Check the bridge is powered on and close to the phone, and that Bluetooth is on. Open the Device tab and tap connect.

The weight on the tag looks wrong

The app prints exactly what the scale sends. Check the scale's unit setting, and use Tare for the box or tray before weighing.

Labels print too small or in the wrong place

In Settings, set Printer Resolution to match your printer (203 or 300 dpi) and check the print direction.

Moving to a new phone

Your licence is tied to one device. Email us with your licence key to move it.
