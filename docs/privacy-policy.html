JBC GS Printer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

JBC GS Printer ("the app") is made by JB Corporation, India ("we", "us"). The app prints jewellery tags and labels using live weight from a weighing scale, through the JBC GS bridge device and a TSC / TSPL label printer. This policy explains what information the app uses, what leaves your device, and the choices you have.

In short: your labels, templates, products and print records stay on your phone. The only information we receive is what is needed to check your licence. We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking, and we do not sell any data.

1. Information sent to us: licence check

The app requires a licence that comes with the JBC GS bridge hardware. To activate and periodically verify the licence, the app sends the following to our licence server (jbcweighingscale.com):

  • Licence key and customer / weighbridge ID that you enter when activating the app.
  • A device identifier: a one-way hash (SHA-256) created on your phone so the licence is tied to one device. On iPhone it is based on a random value stored in the Keychain and the device model. It does not contain your name, phone number, Apple ID or any advertising identifier.

We use this information only to activate your licence, confirm it is valid, and prevent the same licence being used on more devices than allowed. We keep it for as long as your licence is active, and as needed for our business records.

2. Information that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. It is not sent to us:

  • Label templates and designs, including any shop logo you add
  • Products, purity, prices and other item details you enter
  • Print records (serial numbers, weights, dates) and reports
  • App settings such as printer resolution, units and serial numbers

If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted from your phone.

3. Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to connect to the JBC GS bridge, to receive weight readings from your scale and send print jobs to your printer. The app does not use Bluetooth to find your location.

4. Camera and photos

If you choose to add a logo to your labels, the app can take a photo or pick an image from your photo library. The image is converted to a printable logo and stored only in the app on your phone. It is not uploaded.

5. Optional AI label generator

The app has an optional "AI Generate" feature that creates a label layout from a short description you type. It works only if you enter your own Anthropic API key in the app. When you use it, your description and the label size are sent directly from your phone to Anthropic to create the layout. That data is handled under Anthropic's privacy policy and your agreement with Anthropic. We do not receive it. If you do not enter a key, nothing is sent and the app uses a built-in layout generator on your phone instead.

6. Exporting and sharing

When you export reports (PDF, Excel, CSV) or share templates, the file is created on your phone and passed to the app you choose through the system share sheet. Where it goes after that is up to you.

7. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services
  • No location, contacts, microphone or health data
  • We do not sell or rent your information, or share it with third parties for their own purposes

We may disclose information if required by law.

8. Children

The app is a business tool for jewellers and traders. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us what licence information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us at the address below. Deleting licence information will deactivate the licence on that device.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
